    HOW TO PLAY.

    01

    PICK UP A DISK

    Drag the top disk from a tower. You can only move the top disk.

    02

    DROP IT IN PLACE

    Release the disk over another tower. On touch screens, tap a source tower and then a destination.

    03

    REACH THE GOAL

    Move the complete stack to the goal tower. Use hints to reveal an optimal next move.

    THE GOLDEN RULES

    One disk per move. Only the top disk may move. A larger disk may never rest on a smaller disk. In Infinity Towers, the route network can also restrict which towers connect.
